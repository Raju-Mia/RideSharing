### Documentation for Handling Driver Deposits and Stripe Top-Up for Cash Jobs

#### **Objective**
Enable drivers to deposit £250 into your system using Stripe, ensuring transparency, traceability, and feature access (e.g., cash job eligibility). This document outlines the process, Stripe’s role, and considerations for top-ups.

---

### **1. Key Requirements**

1. **Driver Deposit:**
   - Each driver must pay £250 to unlock specific features (like cash jobs).
   - Payments should be trackable and associated with individual drivers.

2. **Stripe Services to Use:**
   - **Stripe Customer Object**: To manage drivers and store their payment methods securely.
   - **PaymentIntent API**: To process payments and ensure compliance with regulations like Strong Customer Authentication (SCA).

3. **Eligibility Check:**
   - Ensure that only drivers who successfully deposit £250 can access the cash job feature.

4. **Top-Up Use Case:**
   - Understand and implement Stripe Top-Up for platform operations, if needed.

---

### **2. Process for Driver Deposits**

#### **Step 1: Driver Registration**
- Register each driver in your system and associate their details (name, email, etc.) with a **Stripe Customer object**.
- This enables you to store their payment methods and process payments later.

#### **Step 2: Adding a Payment Method**
- Use **Stripe.js** to securely collect card details on the frontend.
- Tokenize the payment method and send it to your backend to attach it to the driver’s Stripe Customer object.

#### **Step 3: Processing the £250 Deposit**
- Use Stripe’s **PaymentIntent API** to charge the driver’s card.
- Confirm the payment in real time and handle any errors (e.g., insufficient funds or card declines).

#### **Step 4: Tracking Deposits**
- Store payment details (e.g., `payment_intent_id`, `customer_id`, `amount`) in your database to:
  - Confirm eligibility for features like cash jobs.
  - Maintain a transaction history for compliance and reporting.

#### **Step 5: Feature Activation**
- Once the payment is confirmed (`PaymentIntent.status == "succeeded"`):
  - Mark the driver as eligible for cash jobs in your system.

---

### **3. Stripe Top-Up: Explanation and Use Case**

#### **What is Stripe Top-Up?**
Stripe Top-Up allows the platform owner (your business) to add funds to your Stripe balance manually. This balance is used for platform operations like payouts or refunds.

#### **Use Cases for Top-Up:**
1. **Cover Negative Balances:**
   - If your Stripe balance goes negative (e.g., due to refunds), you can top up to cover it.
2. **Pre-Fund Payouts:**
   - Use top-ups to ensure that driver payouts or refunds are processed without delays.
3. **Manage Internal Operations:**
   - Add funds for operations where you act as the intermediary.

#### **How to Implement Top-Up:**
1. **Create a Top-Up:**
   - Use the Stripe Dashboard or API to add funds to your balance.
2. **Track Top-Up Transactions:**
   - Stripe provides reporting tools to monitor top-ups for accounting purposes.

#### **Top-Up Considerations:**
- **Not Driver-Specific:** Top-ups are for platform use only, not individual users.
- **Separate from Deposits:** Drivers must pay deposits through the regular payment flow, not via top-ups.

---

### **4. Can Stripe Track the £250 Deposit?**

Yes, Stripe can help track deposits in the following ways:

#### **Tracking Through Stripe Dashboard**
1. **Customer and Payment Details:**
   - View all transactions associated with a specific driver (Customer object) in the Stripe Dashboard.
2. **Metadata:**
   - Use metadata to tag payments with additional information (e.g., “Cash Job Deposit”).

#### **Tracking Through Webhooks**
- Implement webhooks (e.g., `payment_intent.succeeded`) to receive real-time updates for deposit payments.
- Store webhook events in your database for historical tracking.

#### **Tracking Through Reports**
- Generate detailed reports in the Stripe Dashboard for:
  - Total deposits collected.
  - Individual driver payments.
  - Reconciliation of payments with features like cash jobs.

---

### **5. Compliance and Security**

1. **Strong Customer Authentication (SCA):**
   - Ensure deposits comply with regulations by using PaymentIntent, which supports SCA.

2. **PCI Compliance:**
   - Use Stripe.js and server-side APIs to handle payment information securely.

3. **Refunds:**
   - Allow refunds for deposits if necessary, using Stripe’s Refund API.

---

### **6. Benefits of Using Stripe for Driver Deposits**

- **Secure Payment Processing:**
  - PCI-compliant and supports global payment methods.
- **Transparent Tracking:**
  - Real-time reporting and webhook integrations.
- **Feature Enablement:**
  - Automated workflows to activate driver features upon successful deposits.

### **7. Summary Workflow**

| Step                      | Action                                                         | Stripe API Used       |
|---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Register Driver           | Create a Customer object                                      | `stripe.Customer`     |
| Add Payment Method        | Attach driver’s card to their Customer account                | `stripe.PaymentMethod`|
| Process £250 Deposit      | Create and confirm a PaymentIntent                            | `stripe.PaymentIntent`|
| Track Payment             | Use metadata and Stripe Dashboard for reporting               | Dashboard/Metadata    |
| Activate Feature          | Update driver status after successful payment                 | Internal Logic        |
